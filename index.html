Frequently asked

Before we get started.

Can you fix an existing WordPress or PHP website?+

Yes. We diagnose and resolve errors, broken layouts, plugin conflicts, checkout failures and integration issues in existing websites.

Do you work with international clients?+

Yes. CoderVex works remotely with businesses worldwide. Project communication and delivery can be handled online.

Can you maintain the website after the initial project?+

Yes. Ongoing support can include updates, backups, routine checks and scheduled technical assistance.

What should I send with my inquiry?+

Your website URL, a short description of the problem or goal, and any useful screenshots or error messages are enough to begin.
